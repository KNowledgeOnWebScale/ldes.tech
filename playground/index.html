Playground

What happens here

A dump is outdated the day after you download it, and an API only answers the questions its publisher foresaw. With an LDES, one publisher shares its data and its changes once, and everyone else replicates them to build their own view and keep it up to date. Here, your browser does that with ldes-client 0.6.1: it fills a substring search and a map while the members stream in. The sync pauses after 1 000 members: Fetch more members reads on. Press Sync again later: your browser remembers where it stopped, and only asks for what changed since.

This playground uses public data only. Requests go directly from your browser to each publisher, so a publisher has to allow cross-origin reads (CORS).

How this page is built

The page bundles ldes-client 0.6.1 for the browser (build script). Entities get their name from schema:name, dct:title, dc:title, skos:prefLabel, rdfs:label, foaf:name and similar properties, and a place on the map from any WKT or GML literal or latitude/longitude pair. The map uses Leaflet and OpenStreetMap tiles. Sharing works like in the inference engine playground: the configuration is stored, compressed, in the part of the URL after the #, which never leaves your browser.
