LDES 1.0 launched at the LDES workshop during **SEMIC 2025 on 25 November in Copenhagen**. The session brought together specification work, working implementations, and plans for adoption across public administrations and cultural heritage.

Pieter Colpaert presented the updated specification after a year of development. The workshop also featured catalog harvesting, a cultural heritage server, water data, a .NET implementation, and RDF-Connect pipelines. Together, these contributions showed how a common publication interface can support different domains and software stacks.

Piotr Sowiński’s workshop keynote connected his work on Jelly with the emerging RDF Messages collaboration, opening a discussion about shared foundations for exchanging RDF streams.

For publishers and consumers, the next step is practical: read the [LDES specification](https://w3id.org/ldes/specification), explore the [implementation reports](https://github.com/SEMICeu/LDES-implementation-reports), and try an [example feed]({{ '/examples/' | relative_url }}).
